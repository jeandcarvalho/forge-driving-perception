% BLOCO 1 - definicao (para a secao de metodo)
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the set of acquisitions and, for $a \in \mathcal{A}$, let
$F_a$ be the frames of acquisition $a$ that carry a valid GPS fix. Frames
without a GPS fix are discarded. Distances are geodesic (haversine) on a sphere
of radius $R = 6\,371\,008.8$~m.

For a frame $f \in F_a$ we define its nearest-neighbour distance
\emph{within its own acquisition}
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{nn}(f) \;=\; \min_{g \in F_a,\; g \neq f} d_{\mathrm{hav}}(f, g),
  \label{eq:nn}
\end{equation}
%
which is defined only when $|F_a| \ge 2$. Accordingly the evaluation universe is
%
\begin{equation}
  U \;=\; \bigl\{\, f \in F_a \;:\; a \in \mathcal{A},\; |F_a| \ge 2 \,\bigr\},
  \label{eq:universe}
\end{equation}
%
so that acquisitions contributing a single geolocated frame are excluded
entirely: they contribute to neither numerator nor denominator. The
near-duplicate rate at threshold $t$ is then
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NDR}(t) \;=\;
  \frac{\bigl|\{\, f \in U \;:\; \mathrm{nn}(f) < t \,\}\bigr|}{|U|}.
  \label{eq:ndr}
\end{equation}
%
Each frame is counted at most once in the numerator: $\mathrm{NDR}$ is a
per-frame statistic, not a pair statistic. Frames are never compared across
acquisitions, so the measure captures redundancy \emph{within} a recording and
never penalises two recordings that revisit the same road. We report
$t = 10$~m and $t = 25$~m.
